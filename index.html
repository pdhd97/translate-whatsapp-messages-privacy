Privacy Policy — Translate WhatsApp Messages

Effective date: November 17, 2026

In short: The extension has no servers of its own. We, the developer, never receive, read, collect, store, sell or share your messages, voice messages, contacts or API keys. The text and audio you choose to translate go directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API, using your own API key, and nowhere else.

1. About this extension

Translate WhatsApp Messages ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that translates conversations on WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com). It translates messages and voice messages you receive into your language, and translates the text you type before you send it. Translations are produced by Google's Gemini API, using an API key that you create and add yourself.

The extension only reads WhatsApp Web pages. It does not read your browsing history, other websites, your contacts list, or files on your computer. Messages that are not translated never leave WhatsApp.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not operate any server that receives your data, and we have no access to it.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party scripts.
  • We do not sell, rent or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, creditworthiness or lending purposes, or for any purpose other than translation.

4. Google Gemini API

Translation requests are processed by Google under the terms of your own Gemini API account. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Depending on your Gemini API plan (for example, the free tier), Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Please review those terms, and don't translate content you consider sensitive if they don't suit you.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Saved translations and original texts stay on your device until you click Clear saved translations in the extension's popup, or uninstall the extension. Older entries are removed automatically when the local limit is reached.
  • You can remove an API key at any time with Remove in the popup.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all data it stored in your browser.
  • Temporary information about API keys that ran out of quota is kept only for the current browser session.

6. Security

All requests to Google are made over HTTPS. Your API key is sent in a request header, never in a web address. The WhatsApp page cannot read the extension's stored data.

7. Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

8. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
